Google Calendar

Not connected

Add, move or delete study sessions in Google Calendar and they become your blocks, here and in the PC app. Timed events only; all-day events are ignored. Syncs every 10 minutes.

    Where do I find the link?
    1. Open calendar.google.com on your computer.
    2. Gear icon, then Settings.
    3. Under "Settings for my calendars", click your calendar.
    4. In "Integrate calendar", copy "Secret address in iCal format".

    Keep it private. Anyone with this link can read your calendar.
