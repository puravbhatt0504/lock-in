IELTS Speaking

3 parts · 11–14 minutes. Start a voice recorder on your phone first. Press Ask the next question and the browser's voice asks it like an examiner; answer out loud before you press again. Then play the recording back and mark it with the speaking band descriptors, listening for fillers, long pauses, repeated words and tense mistakes.
